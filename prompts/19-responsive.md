# 19 — Responsive layout

Already in place: the title EDICT, the room number, one sentence, the room, UNDO, and RESTART.

Do this:

- At 768px and wider, stack them in this order: title and room number, sentence, room, then UNDO and RESTART.
- Under 768px, keep that same stack. Let the sentence wrap onto more than one line. Let the room fill the width of the screen.
- Keep tap targets large enough to hit with a finger.
- Stop the page from scrolling sideways.

Leave out:

- Do not build a separate mobile app, and do not change the rules or the six rooms.

Check:

- Drag the window wider than 768px, then narrower. The sentence stays readable, the room stays fully visible, UNDO and RESTART stay on screen, and there is no horizontal scrollbar.

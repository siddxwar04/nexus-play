# UI polish

Already in place: EDICT, the tagline, one sentence, the room, UNDO, and RESTART. The sentence is the law.

Do this:

- Center EDICT, then the tagline, then the room number, then the sentence.
- Set the sentence in a serif face so it reads as a written law. Keep the controls in a plain sans.
- Make the bracketed word look editable: underline, a quiet highlight, and a soft glow on hover. Replacement words stay words in the sentence, not a settings menu.
- Let the changed word fade and scale slightly.
- Give the room a clearer edge so the marble and the door are easy to see.
- Keep UNDO and RESTART as two quiet controls, with ↶ and ↻.
- When a room is cleared, fade in “Edict Fulfilled” on a small panel. Keep the rule-change count and CONTINUE.

Leave out:

- No new screens, no extra decoration, and no change to how the marble moves.

Check:

- The sentence is the first thing you read. Hover the bracketed word and it glows. The room still fills the page. UNDO and RESTART stay at the bottom.

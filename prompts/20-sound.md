# 20 — Quiet sound

Already in place: a changed word fades into the sentence. Clearing a room shows “Edict Fulfilled”.

Do this:

- Keep that word fade.
- Add four short, quiet cues and nothing else:
  - a soft click when a replacement word is chosen
  - a second, lower cue when that choice changes the rule
  - a cue when the door opens
  - a slightly longer cue when a room is cleared
- Generate them in the browser. Do not add music or audio files.

Leave out:

- No loud hits, no looping music, and no new buttons.

Check:

- Change a word and hear the swap and the rule cue. Open a door and hear the door cue. Clear a room and hear the win cue. The word still fades, and none of the sounds dominate the room.
